# V3 Reference Direction

The user supplied ten screenshots on 2026-09-13, including a repeated pose.
The full-body front view is the primary proportion reference. The closer
three-quarter and raised-arm views guide the helmet, glazing, shoulder armor,
hand construction, exhaust shields and material separation.

## Implemented In This Iteration

- Larger shoulder plates aligned with the upper chest, a narrower abdominal
  grille, tapered silver waist armor and yellow belt inserts.
- Longer titanium thighs and blue calf armor, with a wider robot stance and
  counter-rotated feet that remain level.
- Faceted helmet, tapered antennae, bright angular eyes and folded steel mask.
- Blue mechanical palms and segmented fingers, with visible wrist spindles.
- Blue glazing over internal conduits; continuous, non-overlapping window frames.
- Painted crimson/cobalt metal, brushed steel, titanium and brass surfaces,
  restrained chipped edges and fine roughness variation.
- Rear wheels tucked behind the calves in robot form; connected carriages
  extend sideways before traversing the calf depth and closing into truck form.
- Both reference accessories are visible throughout the robot's 360-degree
  presentation: a dark long-barrel ion rifle and a short orange/gold energon
  axe with hooked blades. The rifle rests in one blue hand and the axe socket
  sits at the other hand; neither is a screen-space overlay.
- Persistent arm-mounted carriers slide out, fold the weapons and raise them
  clear of the truck's front wheels. Telescoping supports remain visible.
  These invented storage mechanisms are not established by the screenshots.
- The elbows slide into the upper-arm rails when folding, keeping the fairings
  above the tire contact plane rather than lifting the truck on arm armor.

The model retains large, readable panels. Extra mesh objects are static details
such as exhaust slots, fasteners, fingers and tread blocks, not independently
flying armor fragments. Robot and truck still use one persistent mesh set.

## Interpretation Limits

The supplied screenshots show the robot, not its complete truck or rear
mechanism. Those areas remain an original simplified design. This is not an
exact commercial-model reconstruction or a proven physical transformation.

A removable core, opening chest doors and the pictured raised-arm action poses
are not added in this iteration. The priorities are body shape, metal finish,
dual-weapon display and keeping the inspection/assembly/transformation workflow functional.
No screenshot, product watermark or third-party texture is used as a rendered
surface. All mesh geometry and PBR maps are generated by the project scripts.
