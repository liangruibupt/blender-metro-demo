## Interpretation Limits

The source is a rendered turntable, not a dimensioned engineering drawing.
Its hidden joints, transformation sequence, load ratings and internal routing
cannot be recovered reliably. Those features are not claimed for this model.
The new shell layouts are original approximations of the observed vocabulary,
not extracted geometry or an exact reconstruction.

The source has a flat cool-gray presentation background. The delivered V2 uses
a continuous darker neutral studio, soft lighting and a visible supporting
floor. `PROMPTS.md` includes both presentation directions.

The reference's surface treatment reads as bright, mostly matte light-gray
clay, not mirror metal. Its stance has more twist and stagger, its crane
projects more obliquely, and its base is more irregular than current V2.
The prompts target those reference characteristics, not the simplified V2
implementation. Suggested focal length, azimuth and frame occupancy are
authoring choices rather than recovered camera metadata. The visible relative
orbit alone does not establish whether the original model or camera rotated.

The user asked to retain only current V2 production assets. Reference originals
are inputs, not obsolete generated versions; they are kept separately. Reference
frames and audio are not inserted into the delivered turntable video.
