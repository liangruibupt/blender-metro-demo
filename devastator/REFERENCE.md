## Interpretation

The first pass targets a G1-like upright humanoid silhouette with dense
construction machinery detail: broad shoulders, a narrower waist, visible
tracks, buckets, truck wheels, mixer drum, cab and crane structure.

These are grayscale sculpt/render references. They do not establish final
paint colors, complete independent vehicle forms, hidden mechanisms, exact
dimensions or transformation paths. Any invented layout in the draft is a
design interpretation, not a sourced engineering claim.
